# Collab Hub — Collective Notebook

The product connects people, projects, organizations and concrete opportunities. The visual direction is a collective project notebook: content has priority over containers, and the interface should make contribution easy to find.

## Identity

Light paper canvas #F8F7F2, dark ink #242E27, working green #28543E, clay red #A14331 and honey in the approved Bia bee artwork. Color communicates action and status. The bee remains a small recognizable signature, not a hero illustration replacing product content.

IBM Plex Serif is used for the public headline, the opportunity-board heading and the feed greeting. IBM Plex Sans remains the operational typeface for navigation, posts, forms and metadata. The display scale is fluid from 40 to 68 px; avoid very tight tracking. Public sections use a shared 1200 px container with 24 px minimum side margins, reduced to 16 px on very narrow screens.

## Structure

The public introduction sits next to real open opportunities on wide screens and above them on mobile. No fabricated metrics or sample listings are needed. The rest of the page explains collaboration through editorial lists and dividers.

Feed sidebars are open rails with ruled sections. The feed greeting and discovery shortcuts use lines and spacing rather than repeated cards. Posts remain distinct white reading surfaces with a lower divider. Buttons and panels use restrained 2–3 px corners; circular people avatars remain recognizable. No decorative gradients, repeated shadows, grid overlays or frosted header.

Preserve all routes, authorization, publication, search, filtering, messaging and notification behavior. The redesign does not add new business claims or send user content.

## References
